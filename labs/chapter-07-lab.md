# Chapter 7 Lab: CI/CD for ML: Databricks Asset Bundles, Jobs, and Automated Retraining

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Author a databricks.yml Asset Bundle defining a multi-task Job (train -> evaluate -> conditionally register) and deploy it to a dev target with databricks bundle deploy, then trigger a run

## Key takeaway

Databricks Asset Bundles are the exam-preferred, source-controlled way to define and promote ML CI/CD pipelines across environments.
