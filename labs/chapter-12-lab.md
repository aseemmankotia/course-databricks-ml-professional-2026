# Chapter 12 Lab: Full Practice Exam Simulation and 120-Minute Test-Day Strategy

**Estimated time:** 120 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a full 60-question, 120-minute timed practice exam simulation spanning Model Development, MLOps, and Model Deployment, then score and review against the answer key

## Key takeaway
