# Chapter 5 Lab: Model Development Scenarios: Choosing the Right Approach Under Exam Pressure

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Work through 10 written exam-style scenarios and classify each into the correct model-development tool/approach, checking answers against a provided answer key

## Key takeaway

Exam scenarios encode the correct tool in specific keywords — training on decoding those keywords is more valuable than memorizing definitions.
