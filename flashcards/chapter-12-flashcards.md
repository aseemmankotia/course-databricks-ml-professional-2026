## Chapter 12: Full Practice Exam Simulation and 120-Minute Test-Day Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Total questions and time limit for this exam's test-day framework? | 60 questions in 120 minutes. Exam tip: baseline pace is ~2 min/question before accounting for review time. |
| 2 | How many minutes should be reserved exclusively for reviewing flagged questions? | 15 minutes at the end. Exam tip: this leaves 105 minutes / 60 questions ≈ 1.75 min/question for your first pass. |
| 3 | First-pass pacing baseline after reserving 15 min for flagged review? | ≈1.75 minutes per question (105 min ÷ 60 questions). Exam tip: don't use the naive 2 min/question figure — it overestimates available first-pass time. |
| 4 | Flag-and-skip strategy: when should you flag instead of solving immediately? | When a dense scenario question threatens to exceed your pacing baseline significantly. Exam tip: make a placeholder guess before flagging — never leave blank. |
| 5 | Domain-weighted review priority order for flagged questions. | Review Model Development and MLOps flagged questions first, then Model Deployment, then Data Prep/lower-weighted domains. Exam tip: prioritize by score impact, not question order. |
| 6 | Elimination technique: fastest way to cut 4 options to 2? | Eliminate options that don't match the question's core category at all (e.g., a correlation metric for a drift-detection question) before comparing the remaining plausible pair. |
| 7 | KS test vs Chi-squared test vs PSI — which fits categorical feature drift? | Chi-squared test (or PSI with categorical bins) — built for categorical/frequency comparisons. KS test is for continuous distributions. Exam tip: match test type to feature type. |
| 8 | Canary vs Blue-Green deployment — key exam differentiator? | Canary routes a small % of traffic to the new model gradually; Blue-Green switches 100% of traffic instantly between two full environments. Exam tip: 'gradual' = canary, 'instant switch' = blue-green. |
| 9 | Keyword cue: 'as it existed at [timestamp]' in a feature engineering scenario. | Signals a point-in-time join is required (Databricks Feature Store) to prevent label leakage from future feature values. Exam tip: near-guaranteed cue phrase. |
| 10 | Remote-proctored exam setup: monitors allowed? | Single monitor only. Exam tip: dual monitors are a common disqualifying mistake — disconnect the second display before starting. |
| 11 | Remote-proctored exam room requirement. | Clear, empty room/desk — no papers, books, secondary devices, or other people. Exam tip: webcam must show the full room during check-in. |
| 12 | What should you do with unanswered questions if time is running low? | Answer every remaining question with your best guess before time expires — never leave a single-answer question blank. Exam tip: blank = guaranteed zero, guess = chance of credit. |
